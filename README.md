## Project Intro/Objective
This project aims to help HorizonTel Communications reduce customer churn through predictive modeling and customer segmentation. Classification models will be developed to identify customers at risk of leaving, while clustering techniques will be used to group customers with similar demographic, service, and billing characteristics.
By combining these approaches, the project seeks to identify key churn patterns, develop actionable customer profiles, and recommend targeted retention strategies to improve customer retention and support long-term business growth.

## Project Description
This project aims to help HorizonTel Communications understand customer churn and develop data-driven customer retention strategies. Using a telecommunications customer dataset containing 7,043 customer records and 33 variables, the analysis examines customer demographics, account tenure, subscribes services, contract types,
billing information, customer lifetime value, and churn behavior. The primary objectives are to identify the factors associated with customer churn, predict which customers are most likely to leave, and develop meaningful customer profiles to support targeted retention initiatives. The methodology combines exploratory data analysis,
data visualization, supervised classification, and unsupervised clustering. Exploratory analysis will examine churn distributions and relationships between customer characteristics and churn outcomes. Classification models models will be developed and compared using metrics such as ROC-AUC, recall, precision, F1 score, and accuracy.
Clustering algorithms will group customers with similar characteristics, enabling comparisons of segment churn patterns, service preferences, and billing behaviors. The results will be combined to identify high-risk customer groups and recommend targeted retention strategies. Key challenges include addressing potential class imbalance,
selecting appropriate features, and translating clustering and predictive results into actionable business recommendations.
